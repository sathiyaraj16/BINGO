How to Play BINGO

1
2 Players & Private Boards

Player 1 and Player 2 each get 25 numbers (1 to 25) shuffled independently. Neither player can see the opponent's board!

2
Strict Private Verbal Calling

When Player 1 calls a number verbally (e.g. "I call 7!"), the card flips to Player 2 with NO visual hint, label, or popup revealing the number. Player 2 must independently find and click 7 on their board. After marking it, Player 2 verbally calls their number to Player 1!

3
Complete 5 Winning Lines

Mark rows (horizontal), columns (vertical), or diagonals. Every completed line strikes out one letter in B-I-N-G-O.

4
Scoring & Winning

Each valid number marked earns +1 point. The first player to cross all 5 letters (5 lines) wins the BINGO championship!
